What you need to know before selecting a GOAXIS service.

What is included in the ₹500 invoice template?

One invoice template design with common customer, item, quantity, rate, tax and total fields. Final file format and customization are confirmed before work begins.

What comes with the ₹800 and ₹1,000 document bundles?

₹800 covers purchase and sales invoice templates. ₹1,000 adds a delivery challan template. They are document layouts rather than automatic accounting or invoicing software.

Does the ₹2,000 website package include domain, hosting and a database?

The advertised package is a website development offer for up to approximately 10 agreed pages, basic contact/enquiry email functionality, a starter management interface, purchase and sales invoices, delivery challans, and domain/hosting for the first 12 months. Document fields, owner permissions, hosting limits, domain availability, renewals and any separately paid providers are confirmed before you order. Enterprise ERP and statutory tax filing are not included.

Will customers and GOAXIS both receive a contact form email?

Yes. Pressing Send stores a reference and attempts to email the enquiry details to GOAXIS and an acknowledgment to the customer, both from contact@goaxis.in. If email sending cannot be confirmed, the page clearly says so and provides the reference.

Will customers and GOAXIS both receive an email?

Yes. Pressing Send saves the enquiry and sends email to GOAXIS plus an acknowledgment to the customer, both from contact@goaxis.in. If the sending provider does not confirm both sends, the form clearly reports this with a reference number.

What happens after I pay?

Cashfree processes payment through hosted checkout. GOAXIS checks the order server-side and sends a payment confirmation email once payment is verified. Project scoping, service delivery and any tax invoice are handled separately.

Can I request a change or refund?

Contact support@goaxis.in with your order reference. Refund eligibility depends on the agreed work and the refund and cancellation policy.

How do I contact GOAXIS?

Email contact@goaxis.in or submit an enquiry. Sales questions can be sent to sales@goaxis.in; invoice questions to billing@goaxis.in.
