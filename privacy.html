Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GOAXIS CONSULTANCY (“GOAXIS”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use goaxis.in, submit an enquiry or make a payment through our website.

1. Information we collect

Enquiries and quotations: when you submit a website form, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, service selected, business name/type and message. Please do not include Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, passwords or other sensitive identity information in an initial enquiry.

Payments: when you use the Pay Now form, we send your name, email, mobile number, payment amount and purpose to our server and to Cashfree Payments to create and verify a payment order. Cashfree hosts the checkout and handles payment-method details such as card information, UPI authentication and other payment credentials. GOAXIS does not ask you to enter your card PIN, UPI PIN or OTP into this website.

Temporary browser storage: the payment page may use session storage in your browser tab to preserve a small set of checkout details for the return/receipt screen. This storage is temporary and is not the authoritative payment record. If browser storage is blocked, checkout should continue; the server-side Cashfree order lookup remains the source of truth.

Technical information: our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process request information such as IP address, browser/user-agent, timestamps and requested URLs as part of delivering and protecting the website. Operational logs are used to troubleshoot the service and protect it from misuse.

2. How enquiry forms work

When you submit an enquiry form, the details are sent to our server and intended to be stored in the Cloudflare D1 database so we can respond. The form also prepares the details in WhatsApp. Your WhatsApp message is not sent until you review it and press Send inside WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of that service.

3. Why we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotations and provide the service you request.
  • To create payment orders, check payment status, handle refunds and resolve payment disputes.
  • To maintain website security, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell customer contact information. We share information only as needed to operate the website and requested services, with service providers involved in hosting, enquiry storage, communications or payment processing, or when disclosure is required by law.

4. Service providers and external services

The website uses Cloudflare for hosting, Worker API execution and enquiry storage. Online checkout and payment-order processing use Cashfree Payments. If you choose WhatsApp, that service receives the message you send. Those providers process information under their own terms and policies; review them before using their services.

5. Retention and security

We keep enquiry and transaction-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide the requested service, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements. The exact retention period may depend on the nature of the enquiry or transaction. We use access controls and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your requests and choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided. We may need to retain limited information where law requires it or where it is reasonably needed for accounting, security or dispute resolution. We may ask for enough information to verify your request before acting on it.

7. Children

Our services are intended for business owners and other adults seeking business or digital support. Please do not submit personal information on behalf of a child without appropriate authority.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
