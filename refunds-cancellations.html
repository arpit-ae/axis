Refunds & Cancellations

We aim to confirm the scope, deliverables, inclusions and price before paid work begins. Contact us promptly if you need to cancel a service or query a payment.

1. Cancellation before work starts

You may request cancellation before work begins. We will review the request against the order or quotation and any payment-provider or third-party charges already incurred. Any refundable amount will be confirmed to you.

2. Cancellation after work has started

If consultation, document review, application preparation, website development or another agreed service has started, a refund may be reduced to reflect work already completed and non-recoverable third-party costs, subject to applicable law and the agreed order.

3. Government registrations and authority decisions

GOAXIS provides assistance and does not control whether a government or regulatory authority approves, rejects, delays or requests changes to an application. A delayed or rejected application does not automatically make completed professional work refundable. Any applicable statutory or third-party refund is governed by the provider or authority involved.

4. Failed, pending or duplicate payments

If money appears to have been debited but the website shows a pending or unconfirmed status, contact us with the order ID and payment date before paying again. We will verify the order status with the payment provider. If you believe you were charged twice, share both transaction references so the issue can be investigated. Refund timing after approval depends on Cashfree and the bank or payment method.

5. How to request a cancellation or refund

Email goaxis.in@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +91 94719 90669. Include your name, order/reference ID, service, payment date and a short explanation. Do not send card numbers, CVV, OTPs or UPI PINs.

Requests are reviewed individually against the agreed scope, work completed, provider status and applicable law. Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable consumer rights under applicable law.
