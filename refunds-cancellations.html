REFUNDS & CANCELLATIONS

Refunds & Cancellations

We aim to keep scope, pricing and timelines clear before work begins.

Cancellation

Requests to cancel a service should be made as soon as possible. If work has not started, a cancellation may be accepted subject to any applicable payment-provider charges.

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the service, the stage of work and whether consultancy, documentation, application preparation or other work has already been performed. Government, statutory, third-party and payment-gateway charges may be non-refundable.

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed to the original payment method. Processing times depend on the payment provider and bank.

Government registrations

GOAXIS provides assistance and does not guarantee approval by any government or statutory authority. Fees paid for completed professional work are not automatically refundable because an authority delays, rejects or requests changes to an application.

Request help

For a cancellation or refund request, contact us with your order/reference details and the reason for the request.
